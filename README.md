EmailConnect by XADI provides EU‑hosted inbound email processing that forwards messages to webhooks. It offers use‑case specific solutions such as enterprise calendar integration, centralized issue tracking, financial document automation, HOA support, and insurance claims intake. The platform emphasizes data residency, AI‑assisted ticket handling, and compliance with EU digital‑sovereignty regulations. Users can try the service for free, explore pricing, and access extensive guides, comparisons, and security documentation.

**Areas:** Company · Email · Webhook · EU · Compliance
